MAGNET Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026 · App: MAGNET (com.magnet.game)

MAGNET is an offline, one-finger physics puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data collected

MAGNET does not automatically collect any data, does not send data to any server, and does not share data with third parties. The app does not request internet access and has no account, sign-in, or personal-information entry. The only exception is the optional feedback email described below, which you start yourself.

Data stored on your device

To work, the game keeps the following only on your device, in the app's own storage; it never leaves the device:

  • Game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best move counts)
  • Selected magnet skin, language and sound/vibration settings
  • Daily level streak (last completed day, streak length)
  • Anonymous in-game counters (e.g. level starts/completions). These stay on the device, are not linked to your identity, and are never transmitted.

Optional feedback

If you tap Settings → Feedback → SEND, your device's email app opens with a draft addressed to the developer. The draft contains an empty space for your comments, the app and Android version, and anonymous gameplay statistics (level reached, stars, per-level start/retry/completion counts). No location, device identifiers, contacts or other personal data are added. You can see, edit or delete the content before sending; nothing is transmitted unless you send it. Emails you send, together with your email address, are used only to answer you and to improve level difficulty, and are not shared with third parties; to have them deleted, write to the address below.

In-app review

When you first finish certain levels, the game may ask Google Play to show its in-app review dialog. The dialog is shown by the Google Play Store app, and Google Play decides whether it appears. MAGNET does not see your rating or review; reviews are subject to Google Play's terms of service.

Advertising

This version shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. If ads are added in the future, this policy will be updated to cover the ad provider's data practices, and the store's data safety information will be updated accordingly.

Children

MAGNET does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

Data deletion

To delete all game data, use Menu → Settings → Reset progress (Turkish interface: Menü → Ayarlar → İlerlemeyi sıfırla) in the game, or uninstall the app. No server-side data exists, so nothing else is needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
