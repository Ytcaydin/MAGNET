MAGNET Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026 · App: MAGNET (com.magnet.game)

MAGNET is an offline, one-finger physics puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data collected

MAGNET does not collect personal data, does not send data to any server, and does not share data with third parties. The app does not request internet access and has no account, sign-in, or personal-information entry.

Data stored on your device

To work, the game keeps the following only on your device, in the app's own storage; it never leaves the device:

  • Game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best move counts)
  • Selected magnet skin and sound/vibration settings
  • Anonymous in-game counters (e.g. level starts/completions). These stay on the device, are not linked to your identity, and are never transmitted.

Advertising

This version shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. If ads are added in the future, this policy will be updated to cover the ad provider's data practices, and the store's data safety information will be updated accordingly.

Children

MAGNET does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

Data deletion

To delete all game data, use Menu → Settings → Reset progress (in the Turkish interface: Menü → Ayarlar → İlerlemeyi sıfırla) in the game, or uninstall the app. No server-side data exists, so nothing else is needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
