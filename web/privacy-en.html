MAGNET Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

MAGNET stores game progress locally on the device. This version does not require an account and does not ask the player to enter personal information.

Advertising

The intended advertising policy allows an interstitial only after every five completed levels. If an advertising SDK is not connected, no advertisement is shown. If a provider is added for distribution, its privacy policy and data-processing terms will also apply.

Children

The game does not require personal information entry. Age targeting and advertising configuration must be completed according to the applicable store and advertising policies before release.

Data deletion

The in-game Settings → Reset Progress action clears locally stored game progress.

Contact

A developer support email must be added before public store submission.
